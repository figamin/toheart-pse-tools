（マルチ　回数イベント　１階１）
（選択肢Ａ　動力源は？）
I suddenly have a spike in curiosity about Multi's source of power.
Robomaids generally run on batteries, I think.
But she's supposed to be the latest model, so maybe she's powered by something else.
I'll ask her about that.
"Hey, Multi?"
"Yes?"
"What are you powered by?"
"My source of power?"
"Yeah."
"Electricity."
Multi answers.
Huh, well that was anticlimactic.
Of course that's what she's powered by.
It's not like a robot eats to draw energy.
"So if electricity powers you, does that mean you're fitted with a battery?"
"Yes.
It's something called a 'lithium battery,' apparently"
"Lithium...?"
"Yes,
it's a more powerful version of the ones used in cellphones and stuff like that."
"Do you sometimes change it?"
"No, I charge it.
About as often as a human would eat."
"Huh.
So if you don't charge yourself in time then you'll stop?"
"I can manage for a while...
Um, I've got something called a secondary fuel cell which lets me generate some of my own power, apparently."
"A fuel cell?"
Another doo-dad I've never heard of.
"I don't quite understand it myself, but I think it produces electricity by combining hydrogen?
And um, oxygen?

To create some kind of reaction...
apparently."
"Hydrogen and oxygen to produce electricity?"
"Uhhh, I think it's something called the electrolysis of water…?"
"Ohhh, I learned about that in middle school.
Something about water splitting into hydrogen and oxygen when you put an electric current into it."
"Yes, that's the one!
My fuel cell does the opposite.
It makes hydrogen react with oxygen to create electricity and water."
"...Interesting. So you've got hydrogen and oxygen inside of you?"
"No, just hydrogen. There's plenty of oxygen around us."
"And that's the source of fuel for your fuel cell then."
"It generates supplementary power when my battery runs out or I do any form of vigorous exercise, apparently."
The fact that she adds "apparently" to the end of everything must mean that she really doesn't know for sure herself either.
"Hey, so you said that you produce both electricity and water, right?
What happens to the water?"
"Oh, that?
It comes out in the form of sweat and..."
"And?"
"And, uh, I-I go to the toilet..."
"Oh, r-right."
She's so human-like, even to the oddest of details.
