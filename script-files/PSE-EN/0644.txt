It's lunch time.
I go over to the library again.
I'm not there for my usual nap, but for a different reason this time.
It's to see Multi charging her battery.
Multi is in an empty corner of the library, quietly asleep as she charges her battery.
She's a robot, yet she sleeps.
And she has dreams.
I wonder what kind of dream she's having right now.
I gently stroke Multi's head.
"...Sweet dreams."
I whisper to her, and then...
"<name>-san…"
She suddenly says my name.
"Huh?"
I take a closer look at her, but she's still asleep.
The progress bar on her screen is still only at 58%.
There's no way she's awake.
Does that mean...
she was muttering in her sleep just now?
"…"
Multi has a look of joy on her face as she sleeps.
Maybe she's having a dream with me in it.
I wonder what's happening in the dream.
