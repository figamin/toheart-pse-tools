（昼休み）
（食堂）
The student store becomes a tiny battlefield at lunch time.
Bloodthirsty savages with bloodshot eyes attack like a swarm unleashed to be the first to feast on the delicious sandwiches.
Masashi and I successfully procure the goods in question just before the onslaught begins.
"Man,
the battle for food is rough."
I watch the brutal battle unfold before me as I take a bite of a pork cutlet sandwich that the savages are longing so desperately for.
"A winner with his spoils just sitting back and enjoying the show, eh?"
Masashi has also gotten his hands on the sandwich he was after.
The battle intensifies and rages on.
Freshman, juniors, and seniors. Guys and girls. It doesn't matter on this battlefield.
Snarling barbarians wade through swarms of people to grab their sandwiches of choice.
"They really should've planned their course of attack better."
A relaxed smile spreads across my face as I feign exasperation.
As I ball up the wrapper of my pork cutlet sandwich and throw it in the trash can…
"E-Excuse meee!
I'd like two pork cutlet sandwiches, one curry bun, one yakisoba roll and…"
I hear a familiar voice amongst the raging battlefield.
"Um, one cream bun, two, uhh…sausage rolls, and… GAH!"
And who gets launched out from the bloodfest? Multi, that's who.
Not letting the knockback discourage her, she reaffirms her resolve and leaps back into the crowd.
"T-Two sausage rolls, one jam bun, and a bear plushie!"
"Go do your shopping at a department store!"
"Huh?
Wh-What?!"
Multi gets a chilly reception from the lady at the student store.
"There's no point in reading out your little list!
You grab it first and it's yours! Those are the rules here!"
"O-Okay! Understood!"
Even their little back-and-forth gets stamped out by the uproar of the raging battle.
Looks like Multi's been turned into an errand girl.
"Um... This one, this one, this one, and..."
*whump*
"Aah;"
Multi gets bumped and is thrown out of the swarm again.
"Aww..."
Yet she refuses to give up and wriggles back into the battle.
"Um, uhh... This one, this one, this one, this one, and..."
*whump*
"Aah;"
*whump*
"Gahhh;"
*whump*
"Wahhh;"
"Aww, I-I can't get the sandwiches;"
Tch. Guess a man's gotta do what a man's gotta do.
I can't watch this any longer.
"Hey, Multi."
"Aww… <name>-san…"
"C'mon, gimme that list."
"Oh..."
I take the list of orders out of Multi's hand.
"Let's see.
Pork cutlet sandwich, curry bun, and…
A bear plushie?
Who's the wise guy who added this?!
Writing whatever they want like it's their family shopping list. Jerks.
Listen, Multi, how many things have you bought already?"
"Just this..."
"You haven't even gotten half of what's on this list.
Ugh, fine. I'll do the rest."
"Aww… <name>-san…"
I burst into the raging battle and gather the remaining buns and sandwiches, albeit somewhat forcefully.
"Ma'am!
I'm getting all of this and the stuff she has too."
"Sure, that comes to…2,450 yen!"
"Hey, Multi! The money!
Give me the moneeeeey!"
"R-Right!"
*jingle, jangle*
"One, two, three... Yep, that's perfect."
I grab Multi and escape from the swarm of savages.
"Th-Thank you sooooooo much!
I managed to get all of the buns and sandwiches thanks to you!"
She bows so deeply that her forehead is practically touching her knees.
"I was so close to giving up...
Now I can head back to my classmates without any fear!"
"Listen, Multi… Are your classmates forcing you to be their lackey?"
"Lackey?"
"Someone who's forced to run errands-just like you right now.
It's what they call people who are taken advantage of like that."
"But, I'm a Robomaid, so it only makes sense for me to be of use to everyone."
"Well yeah, but...
Ugh, Robomaids are such people-pleasers.
Have you been programmed to never go against a human's orders?"
"No, I'm not programmed to do anything."
"Then why would you put yourself through such an ordeal to work for others?
Don't you think it's ridiculous?"
A smile spreads across Multi's face.
"I love working and, more importantly, I love seeing looks of joy on people's faces.
Seeing others happy makes me extremely happy."
"…"
Honestly, I feel some feelings myself when I see Multi's smile.
I was completely under the impression that Multi worked for humans because she's a Robomaid.
I assumed that it was a given that robots worked for humans, so they did so without a single complaint.
But it looks like these assumptions were wrong.
Multi doesn't work because she's a Robomaid.
She works with such a bright smile on her face because she loves humans from the bottom of her heart and genuinely wants to be of use to them.
...I could never do anything like that.
"Thank you very much for helping me again.
You always do so much for me that I don't know how to even thank you…"
"It's really no big deal. Don't mention it.
More importantly, you're heading back to your classroom now, right?
I'll carry half of all that since it's way too much for one person."
"I-I couldn't possibly trouble you to do that!"
"It's totally fine. I don't mind.
I've already come this far so at least let me see this out to the end."
"Thank you…"
"Okay, let's go."
"Oh, <name>-san."
"Hm?
What's up?"
"Um, well..."
"Well what?"
"I had to buy drinks too!"
"WHAT THE HEEEEEECK?!"
